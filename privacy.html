Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Ksenia Pipeline ("the app") is a private tool used only by the operator of the YouTube channel «Ксения в Кёльне» (@ksenia_koeln) to upload the operator's own videos. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube (scopes youtube.upload, youtube.readonly): uploading videos to the authorized channel and reading that channel's basic information (channel name and ID) to confirm the correct channel is selected.
  • Google Drive (scope drive): creating folders and storing the operator's own video files, images and text notes in the operator's own Drive.

The app does not access, collect or process data of any other users.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to perform the actions above on the operator's own accounts.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer and are never transmitted to third parties.
  • The app does not sell, share, rent or transfer any data to third parties and does not use data for advertising.
  • The app has no servers and stores no data outside the operator's computer, YouTube and Google Drive.

How to revoke access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored tokens are deleted by removing the app's credentials folder.
