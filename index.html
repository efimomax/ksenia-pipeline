Ksenia Pipeline

Ksenia Pipeline is a private, single-user tool used by the operator of the YouTube channel «Ксения в Кёльне» (@ksenia_koeln). The channel publishes short videos that teach everyday German words with a clearly labeled AI-generated character.

The tool runs on the operator's own computer. It prepares the operator's own videos and uploads them to the operator's own YouTube channel and Google Drive, using the YouTube Data API and the Google Drive API. It is not offered to other users.
